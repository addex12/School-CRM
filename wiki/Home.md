## Overview

**School CRM** is a modern, open-source School Management and CRM system for educational institutions.  
It helps manage users, surveys, tickets, feedback, announcements, knowledge base, and more.

- **Tech Stack:** PHP 7.4+, MySQL/MariaDB, JavaScript, Node.js/Express (AI/API), HTML5, CSS3

---

## Features

- Role-based user management (Admin, Teacher, Student, Parent, etc.)
- Survey builder and analytics
- Ticketing and support system
- Internal messaging and announcements
- ERP-style dashboards and analytics
- Classes, sections, and curriculum management
- Audit logs and backup/restore
- Modular, extensible codebase
